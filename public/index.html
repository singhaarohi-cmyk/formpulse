Connect your Google Form

This dashboard is running on your computer (localhost). Google cannot reach it from the internet yet. Deploy it (see README) or run a tunnel such as npx localtunnel --port 3100, then open the dashboard from that public address and copy the script again.
  1. Open your Google Form, click the three dots (top right), then Apps script.
  2. Delete everything in the editor, paste the script below, and click Save.
  3. Pick setup in the function dropdown and click Run. Approve the permissions.
  4. Optional: pick syncAll and click Run to import responses that already exist. If the form already has 50+ responses this will fire the matching alerts right away.
